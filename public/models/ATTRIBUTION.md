The GLB embeds the same author, source and license in asset.extras.
Current adaptation: the emblem mesh is reused with a modified material and placement.
The chassis, camera islands, optical assemblies, interactive screens and foldable hinge
are custom procedural geometry, rebuilt from user-supplied visual references.
The original GLB remains unchanged. This is an unofficial design concept, not an official iPhone 18 model.
